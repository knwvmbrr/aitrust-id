Personal and organization access
AI TRUST ID · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-08

Free personal capabilities and the separate organization scope.

Free personal tools
Personal detection methods, evidence and verification capabilities are free and open under the project’s contribution invariant. The PS device preview needs no account, extension or subscription. Future personal tags retain the same rule and must independently pass their release gate.

Person catalogue: /#person
Contribution invariant: /reference/contributing-policy.md

Organization and proprietary workflows
Fleet deployment, host capture, audit/retention, SSO, contracted support and organization-specific policy packages are preserved scope. They have their own delivery, security and acceptance requirements. Enterprise access must not change a public tag’s meaning or hide personal evidence. There is currently no checkout, paid contract, advertised price or service SLA for these offerings.

Enterprise catalogue: /#enterprise
Complete scope: /#scope

Before paid service activation
Publish the actual service scope, pricing, cancellation/refund rules, data processing and retention terms, support commitments and accountable legal provider before taking an order. Those terms must match the delivered service; this policy does not invent a billing model or a paid promise.
