Contributing and accessibility
AI TRUST ID · Version 1.7 · Effective 2026-10-10
Published by AI Trust ID.

Useful outcomes, open evidence and accessible release gates.

Choose a bounded outcome
Pick a tag, defect or delivery route and describe a reproducible result. Review source, dependencies and acceptance criteria before editing. Submit focused code changes with checks and documentation; sign off contributions under the project’s DCO rule. No CLA is required by the contribution document.

Choose a contribution: /#assist
Contribution rules: /reference/contributing-policy.md

Keep personal capabilities open
Do not add a metered inference dependency, automatic content collection or a paid gate around personal detection/evidence. Accessibility is a release gate. A detector may not claim more confidence than its evidence supports. Organization administration can be offered separately without weakening the personal method.

Independent and accessible review
Reviewers label without seeing the detector’s predictions and record uncertainty and disagreement. Review files return privately to their coordinator. Automated accessibility checks complement human screen-reader and real-device checks; they do not substitute for them.
