Dependency transparency
The public repository preserves upstream notice texts and the package or image versions inspected. The collection covers two complete npm locks, including platform alternatives, and three Linux staging images. Finding a notice does not finish a license review: bundled code, source obligations and other assets remain recorded gaps. You can inspect and check this evidence without submitting data.

Code and specification
Apache-2.0 governs project code, including commercial use subject to its conditions. CC BY 4.0 governs the specification and requires appropriate attribution when sharing/adapting it. The licenses have their own warranty and rights limitations; a public repository is not a blanket license to every third-party asset or trademark.
