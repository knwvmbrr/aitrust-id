AI TRUST ID — Native preview privacy

Your choice, your text
Paste or share text, then choose Check with PS. Selecting or sharing text does not start a check. This preview checks only PS's supported command-execution patterns. It does not redact personal information, verify every statement, identify who wrote it or provide a safety clearance. Independent accuracy validation remains pending.

Processing and storage
This checker processes selected text on this device using bundled code. It does not send your text or results to AI Trust ID, an AI provider, a research database or an analytics service. It requires no account, tracks no advertising identifier and includes no advertising or research-submission interface. The checker and its notices work without an internet connection after installation.

Text and results remain in the current view until you edit or clear them, dismiss the Share checker, or move the app to the background. Editing invalidates the previous result. There is no in-app conversation history. Your source app, keyboard, dictation service, device backups, screenshots and operating-system services have their own behavior and settings; this notice is not a device-wide privacy guarantee.

Beta feedback and help
Apple's TestFlight feedback and diagnostics, if you choose to use them, are handled separately by Apple. Do not include private text or screenshots in feedback. Nothing in this app automatically forwards feedback or selected text into our research pipeline.

The public privacy policy and support information are at https://aitrustid.com/policies/privacy/ and https://aitrustid.com/reference/operate/. Opening a website uses your browser's connection and that website's policies. This view does not open either website or send selected text.

Open source
The AI Trust ID implementation is available at https://github.com/knwvmbrr/aitrust-id under Apache-2.0. The bundled Python, Pyodide and Unicode components have separate notices below. Bundled source/code identity and working tests do not establish independent accuracy.
