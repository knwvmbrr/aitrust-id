# Public terms, policies and validation language

Publish current operating policies as readable, crawlable pages with version,
effective date, downloadable text and links to the governing source/license.
The Legal panel is the index; every policy has its own address and no JavaScript
requirement. Keep the compact tag catalogue unchanged.

Source: `site/src/policies.js`, consumed by the Legal panel and static renderer.
The build emits the complete policy text and adds canonical policy pages to the
sitemap. Existing security, governance, conduct, contribution and accessibility
sources remain inspectable. Terms describe the current free preview and existing
licenses; they do not invent a corporation, paid subscription, arbitration clause,
training grant, SLA or certification credential.

Use the positive release rule everywhere: each tag must pass its own published
validation gate before release. Each tag separately displays current engineering,
independent accuracy and human accessibility evidence. Do not turn the policy into
a claim that unfinished independent review is already complete. A working preview
is labeled as a preview; formal third-party certification is a different scope.

Risks: contradictory licenses, hidden collection, inaccurate promises, stale copies,
blanket validation claims and inaccessible legal text. Mitigate with a shared
source, explicit current/future boundaries, real links, no automatic submissions,
rendered/no-script checks and a test that preserves PS's failing accuracy gate.
Public policies expose decisions and rights, never credentials or vulnerability
payloads. Private reports can lead to safe public summaries.

Verify metadata and sitemap uniqueness, policy HTML/text agreement, source links,
320px and 200% reflow, both themes, keyboard/axe checks, all tag/workflow regressions
and unchanged device method. Deploy the reviewed build and verify public hashes.
