# Proposed legal and operational corrections — draft 0.1

Reviewed by Codex, 2026-10-10. These clauses correct identified defects in the
source drafts. They are not filed articles, adopted bylaws, an executed contract,
a tax opinion, or a lawyer's clearance. Current website policies govern only the
actual preview. Michael approves business choices; qualified counsel reviews legal
applicability and final drafting. Original source hashes are in `source-inventory.json`.

## 1. Public participants and statutory members

Replace blanket statements that all non-voting members lack inspection or
other statutory rights with:

> Community participants, reviewers and contributors are not corporate members
> merely because they participate. A statutory membership class may be created
> only through a legally reviewed, duly adopted provision identifying its rights.
> Nothing in this policy purports to eliminate a right that applicable law grants
> to a person who is actually a corporate member.

This is a proposed repair to the Articles §7.1 / Bylaws §3.6 and §8.1.1
inconsistency. It does not silently adopt a different membership structure.
Michigan statutory applicability still needs current authoritative legal review.

## 2. Independence and transaction-specific conflicts

> A general independent-director classification does not establish eligibility
> for a particular transaction. For a transaction requiring the draft's two-person
> unconflicted authorized body, approval is blocked when fewer than two eligible
> members remain. A vacancy or recusal does not waive that minimum. Replace the
> eligible reviewer or defer the transaction; record the conflict and action.

Delete the claim that a two-independent-director body survives losing one while
retaining two eligible approvers. Preserve prior approval, comparable information
and timely records. IRS compensation procedure and annual Form 990 independence
are separate tests; neither is an automatic finding of reasonable compensation.

## 3. Reproducibility with lawful confidentiality

> Publish methods, versions, reproducible lawful fixtures, findings and limitations.
> Do not commit secrets, private submissions, personal dossiers, privileged material,
> unlicensed data or an active independent holdout to a public repository. Record
> restricted evidence's version, access basis and review procedure in a protected
> register. Publish a sanitized or synthetic reproducer where possible. If full
> verification is unavailable, identify that limit rather than claiming it passed.
> An AI-generated counterexample receives a proposed label; a responsible reviewer
> establishes its expected result before it becomes an authoritative fixture.
> Rerunning a commercial-model prompt may produce different output. A stored prompt
> and response support auditability, not guaranteed identical regeneration or truth.

This replaces the universal public-commit and expected-label rule in Doc13 §3.4.
Public protocol conformance vectors and a protected independent accuracy holdout
are separate evidence collections.

## 4. Tag-specific validation and certification

> Each tag has its own claim, input, method, measured failure modes and release gate.
> A protocol conformance pass does not establish detector accuracy, authorship,
> safety, revocation freshness or accessibility. A media-transformation study
> cannot discharge a text-origin tag's validation. Publish the demonstrated scope
> and unresolved gates separately. No certification is issued before its governing
> body, applicable standard, lawful mark use and actual review are operating.

Future certification deadlines, fees, renewals and appeal promises remain proposed
until an accountable operator accepts and can deliver them. Ordinary product
branding and a mark certifying others' goods/services require distinct analysis;
the owner-use limitation is not a blanket ban on every commercial activity.

## 5. Research contribution and retained rights

> Basic checking remains available without a signup or research contribution.
> The first pilot requests structured tag-result, method-version, failure-category
> and usability feedback only. A local file is not a submission. No permission to
> collect examples, publish content or train a model arises from using the tool,
> downloading it, posting a public issue, or consenting to a different purpose.
> Contributors retain their rights. Any later research contribution must identify
> its precise permitted purpose, access, retention, withdrawal and backup handling
> before acceptance. Example sharing needs a separate preview, redaction review,
> authority to share and specific permission. No broad perpetual content license,
> advertising reuse, data sale or unrelated model training is granted by this draft.

A new purpose requires a new prospective choice; rewriting terms does not expand
permission for old data. Do not promise anonymity, complete redaction, recovery
or deletion from externally shared copies. Future training must track provenance
and removals; do not promise that deleting a row untrains a deployed model.

## 6. Separate retention classes and release prerequisites

> Corporate minutes, tax/employment records, certification decisions and voluntary
> research material have separate retention schedules and access controls. The
> draft's ten-year certification schedule is not the research default. No real
> research intake launches until its active-store period, backup expiry, deletion
> procedure, accountable access roles and replacement-host recovery are approved
> and exercised. A deletion request must not require disclosure of a private answer
> or unnecessary identity documents merely to locate a contribution.

The durations have not been selected. A no-data pilot preparation does not imply
that future retention and deletion already function.

## 7. Entity, tax and compensation decisions

> Evaluate the actual three-year activities, beneficiaries, income, expenditure,
> public-support assumptions, services and recurring compliance costs before
> choosing an exemption or subsidiary. Donations versus service fees alone do not
> determine eligibility. Do not describe every expense as deductible against every
> type of income, or every organizational conversion as universally irreversible.
> A template is not authorization to form an entity or move its assets.

An equity-allocation formula must publish its actual denominator and exceptions.
A promise based on a net-defined base is not a promise of the same percentage of
gross revenue. Those owner business choices remain unresolved.

## 8. Transparent records with protected individuals

> Publish effective policies, draft status, public decisions, conflicts and evidence
> within lawful limits. Keep credentials, private complaints, security reports,
> protected reviewer identities and personal legal background restricted. List the
> category and reason for restricted records without exposing their substance.
> Public community posting and private research consent remain separate workflows.

No professional review, formation, exemption, trademark clearance, independent
board, staffed response deadline or operating certification is claimed here.
