# Control compensation taxes and certification

## Control and payment

For A, an adopted single-member arrangement can concentrate business decisions and the economic interest in you. Existing licenses, contracts, laws and obligations still constrain what the Company can do. Company finances and private funds stay separate.

For B, a founder may hold governance powers under valid instruments, but governance control is not personal ownership of charitable assets. You cannot treat the parent or its subsidiary as a personal profit-distribution account. A 501(c)(3) may pay for real services; the amount, decision makers, comparability evidence and documentation matter. The IRS describes an advance, unconflicted approval procedure supporting a rebuttable presumption of reasonable compensation [C4–C5]. It does not guarantee that a particular salary is lawful.

## Tax and reporting

A single-member LLC is generally disregarded for federal income tax unless a different classification is elected; employment and certain excise taxes have separate treatment [C6]. Obtain advice for the actual owner and activities. This comparison makes no S-corporation election or tax-saving claim.

A disregarded LLC under a nonprofit is generally included with its parent for federal reporting. The IRS Form 990 instructions still require relevant subsidiary information and activities. A legal liability envelope does not remove unrelated business income tax or turn an unrelated activity into a charitable one [C7–C8]. Do not reuse the historical promise of zero additional federal filing burden as an unconditional commitment.

Formation alone does not establish 501(c)(3) recognition, public-charity classification, deductible gifts or grant eligibility. Actual activity, public benefit, finances and applicable requirements must support the chosen status.

## Certification and commercial work

A product brand and a certification mark are different. USPTO describes a certification mark as certifying others against standards; an ordinary trademark identifies a commercial source [C9]. Counsel must analyze the actual certified goods/services, owner-use restrictions, control and conflicts. Neither a founder LLC nor a foundation label automatically solves that issue.

A possible later alternative is an independently governed standards nonprofit alongside your separately owned commercial LLC. **That is different from a foundation-owned LLC.** It needs actual separation, contracts, fair dealings and conflict handling. It is not selected here and should not be built solely to make an unvalidated certification claim sound independent.
