## Filing sequence

Confirm structure, legal name, resident agent, addresses and management. Review the completed official form, public information and filing charge. Submit through the current LARA portal at https://www.michigan.gov/fileonline. Save the accepted, filed Articles, entity identification number and actual effective date. An application submission or payment alone is not the accepted filing.

After acceptance, obtain an EIN directly from the IRS when needed for the company's banking/tax operations; the IRS application is free and advises forming the state entity first [M5]. The owner supplies any required SSN/ITIN privately. This document neither collects it nor applies for the EIN.

Then execute the operating agreement and initial action, open separate banking and reconcile asset rights and policy/provider identity. The new company does not silently replace the individual on existing contracts or obtain ownership of existing assets.

# Operating agreement management and finances

## 5 Management and authority

The Company is member-managed, consistent with its Articles. The Member directs ordinary operations and may sign Company contracts in the Company's exact legal name and his capacity as Member. A contractor, employee, AI tool or account administrator has only the authority specifically delegated in writing. Access credentials do not confer authority to borrow, transfer IP, admit members or sign legal representations.

The Member's written approval is required for a new owner, change of tax election, amendment, material borrowing or guarantee, sale of substantially all assets, change of management, merger, dissolution, related-party transaction or material IP transfer. Record the decision and supporting information. These internal restrictions do not purport to defeat third-party rights arising under applicable agency law.

## 6 Contributions and separate finances

Record the initial contribution, date and basis in Schedule A. No amount or asset is contributed merely by an empty schedule. Further contributions or documented Member loans require a written record distinguishing debt from equity. There is no automatic promise of future funding.

Maintain separate Company bank accounts and books. Do not commingle Company and personal or unrelated-project funds. Document business expenses, reimbursement and related-party transactions. No member loan or advance has unstated interest or repayment terms. The Member may authorize payment services only after the actual provider, deliverable, price, cancellation/refund terms and data responsibilities are established.

## 7 Allocations distributions and taxes

Subject to applicable law and any valid tax election, Company economic results belong to the sole Member. Distributions occur only as authorized by the Member and permitted by applicable law after considering liabilities, taxes and operating needs. Do not distribute assets needed to satisfy lawful obligations.

Have a tax professional confirm federal and state classification, filings, elections, estimated payments, payroll and sales/use-tax obligations for the actual activity. This Agreement makes no S-corporation or other tax election and promises no tax savings. Keep supporting records and the accepted EIN notice privately. A single-member LLC does not automatically become tax exempt.

## 8 Standard of conduct and protection

Perform duties subject to applicable Michigan law. No clause excuses unlawful conduct, breaches nonwaivable duties or guarantees personal immunity. Company indemnification, advancement and insurance require a lawful, documented determination of eligibility and available funds. This draft does not grant blanket indemnity or release personal guarantees, individual misconduct or pre-formation obligations. Obtain qualified advice before adopting additional liability limitations.

# Operating agreement records IP and continuity

## 9 Records confidentiality and public accountability

Keep the Articles, this Agreement and amendments, ownership/contribution records, material consents, financial and tax records, contracts, licenses and required statutory records. Maintain required access and retention under applicable law. Publish accurate product policies and sanitized decisions while restricting private addresses, signatures, tax IDs, credentials, security reports, participant data and protected evidence. Transparency is not permission to disclose another person's rights or confidential information.

## 10 Existing intellectual property and new work

Existing founder, contributor and third-party assets remain with their actual owners unless Schedule B and a separate signed instrument establish a specific transfer or license. Merely forming the Company does not assign the domain, repository, copyright, mark, server contract or user data. Preserve open-source notices and third-party rights. Identify human and AI assistance accurately when relevant; do not claim rights the Company cannot establish.

New contractor work needs a signed agreement specifying scope, payment, ownership/license and third-party materials before incorporation into the product. This Agreement does not retroactively transfer a contractor's work. Any future foundation, certifying organization or proprietary assistant requires its own ownership, conflicts and data-rights analysis.

## 11 Transfers and new members

The Member may transfer interests only through a lawful written instrument, with tax, contractual and governance consequences reviewed and Company records updated. No additional member is admitted without written admission and an amended agreement addressing multiple owners. A purported transfer of an economic interest is not automatically admission as a managing member. No investor rights, vesting or buy-sell price are invented here.

## 12 Death incapacity and continuity

Record an actual estate/continuity plan and designated contact privately in Schedule C. Do not assume a family member, executor or agent automatically becomes the managing member. Admission, lawful representation and continuation follow applicable law and valid documents. Review the plan with estate counsel so it addresses incapacity, ownership and timely continuation. No unsigned designation in this packet grants power of attorney or authority over credentials.

## 13 Dissolution and winding up

The Company dissolves upon an applicable legal event or a lawful written Member decision. An authorized person winds up, resolves or provides for obligations, handles data and contracts lawfully, makes required filings and distributes remaining assets in the lawful order. Keep required records and a responsible contact after shutdown. Do not abandon participant data or export deletion duties when the service closes.

# Compliance calendar and official sources

## Recurring obligations

Michigan LLC annual statements are generally due February 15. A company formed after September 30 need not file on the immediately following February 15 [M3]. If formation actually occurs in November 2026, that rule would make the first ordinary statement due February 15, 2028; enter the date only after formation is confirmed. Recheck the fee at filing because the statute changes it after September 30, 2027 [M2].

Calendar tax returns and payments with the actual tax adviser, not an assumed tax election. Review permits, resident-agent information, insurance, ownership records, contracts, dependency notices, access and recovery regularly. FinCEN's current BOI guidance exempts US-created entities; recheck the rule at formation rather than following an old blanket filing instruction [M7]. No BOI submission is authorized here.
