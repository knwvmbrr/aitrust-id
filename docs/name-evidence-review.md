This records observations, not trademark clearance, legal priority or a filing

## Corrections to the pasted advice

| Statement | Evidence and limit |
| --- | --- |
| The AI Lab launched TIP on October 5, 2026 | Its current announcement displays **May 26, 2026**. That contradicts the asserted date; a page's displayed date alone does not prove when it first appeared or when services first entered commerce. |
| The public AI Trust ID repo predates October 5 | GitHub reports this public repository was created **2026-10-08T17:39:02Z**. Earlier local work and domain acquisition are separate evidence; they were not established as commercial use by this check. |
| Domain/repo dates establish priority | USPTO distinguishes first use anywhere from use in commerce. A purchase, local file, or repository timestamp by itself does not demonstrate that the relevant goods/services were offered under the mark in qualifying commerce. |
| Nobody has a registration because both use ™ | A symbol is not a clearance search. USPTO explains that ™ can be used without an application; ® is restricted to registered goods/services. Symbols do not establish the absence of registrations, applications or other rights. |
| C2PA adoption has not changed in eight years since 2018 | C2PA's founding announcement is dated **February 22, 2021**. Comparing earlier general metadata to C2PA adoption conflates different populations and technologies. The quoted 0.5%/7.2% rates were not verified from an identified primary dataset with a denominator and sampling method. |
| No Wayback capture means no earlier use | Absence from one archive is not evidence that a service was absent elsewhere. No archive completeness or historical use was established. |
| Keeping the site live necessarily preserves priority, or taking it down necessarily destroys it | Neither legal conclusion follows from the timestamp checks. Maintenance of this engineering preview is not a legal clearance opinion. |

Current page observations can be edited or backdated. An application, a registration,
first use, similarity and likelihood of confusion are distinct questions. This
review does not verify the current Tumeryk application status or conclude that
another party copied the project. A current official search and qualified review
remain necessary for a legal conclusion.
