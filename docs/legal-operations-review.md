## Standards used

The FTC warns against silently expanding old privacy commitments through retroactive
terms changes. New research uses must have a new prospective choice; public posting
is not blanket training consent. [FTC privacy commitments](https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2024/02/ai-other-companies-quietly-changing-your-terms-service-could-be-unfair-or-deceptive).

Minimize what is collected, inventory actual flows, limit access, set a real
retention/disposal schedule and plan incident response. The metadata-first export
omits examples and identifiers; it does not establish future server controls.
[FTC data protection guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business).

For the IRS rebuttable-presumption procedure, prior approval by an eligible body,
comparability and timely documentation matter. Annual independence does not replace
transaction-specific eligibility. [IRS compensation procedure](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/rebuttable-presumption-intermediate-sanctions).

Primarily performing particular services is incompatible with the business-league
exemption described by the IRS; certification-fee revenue alone establishes no
classification. [IRS business-league services](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/other-non-profits/performing-particular-services-business-league-internal-revenue-code-section-501c6).

Certification marks certify authorized others' goods/services. Analyze that use
separately from ordinary product branding; intent-to-use applications and proof of
actual qualifying use have different stages. This review does not decide the name.
[USPTO certification applications](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/certification-mark-applications).

Automated accessibility tools cannot establish conformance alone. Physical Safari,
VoiceOver, TalkBack and knowledgeable human evaluation remain acceptance tasks.
[W3C accessibility evaluation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/).

The Michigan archive URL attempted for inspection law was unavailable. The member
clause finding above is a source-draft contradiction, not an independently verified
opinion about enforceability under current Michigan law. Qualified current-law
review remains open. Negative search results do not establish legal immunity.

## Collection and signup decision

Basic use stays account-free. Optional local exports remain possible in open-source
software; a person can decline sharing or change the client. That is consistent
with the purpose of the product. Browser hosting necessarily exposes requests to
its hosting provider, but those logs are not tag outcomes or permission to ingest
answers. Do not convert them into a research corpus.

An optional future contributor session/deletion receipt could support withdrawal;
community authors, reviewers and administrators need different permissions.
No new endpoint is authorized by this document. Before implementation, choose
retention and backup expiry, approve a concrete intake contract, restrict roles,
and prove deletion/recovery with synthetic data. A prediction or usability report
must never be relabelled as independent ground truth.
