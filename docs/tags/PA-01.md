# PA-01 — Partial AI contribution provenance

Planning contract · 2026-10-08. No new runtime support, registration or release is asserted.

## Baseline job and outcome

Describe supported AI contribution and subsequent human modification within the observed workflow.

The reader can distinguish documented contribution/edit events from unobserved authorship.

## Inputs, outputs and boundaries

**Inputs:** Consented composition evidence and artifact binding.

**Outputs:** Documented AI-contribution and modification evidence bound to subject revisions and the observed workflow.

**Limits:** No negative verdict from absent human evidence; Timing must be suppressed for assistive input; ordinary templates are not automation proof; Raw event privacy and signer integrity need independent review

## Method and evidence development

- Separate explicit generation/edit evidence from behavioral research hypotheses.
- Bind events to subject revisions while suppressing unsuitable timing signals for assistive input.
- Evaluate dictation, IME, paste, templates, interruption and unavailable history without identity profiling.

## Acceptance and error boundaries

- Behavioral indicators alone must first demonstrate that they support the bounded claim.
- No per-person timing template or absent-human inference.
- Do not use outside knowledge as proof of human authorship.

Numeric thresholds, supported languages/subjects and hardware/latency requirements are defined and accepted for this claim before independent evaluation; no PS threshold is borrowed by default. Existing editorial floors are not measured accuracy.
