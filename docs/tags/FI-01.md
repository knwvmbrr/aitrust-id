# FI-01 — Reference contradiction and citation problems

Planning contract · 2026-10-08. No new runtime support, registration or release is asserted.

## Baseline job and outcome

Distinguish contradicted claims from citations that cannot be resolved within the declared reference set.

The reader can inspect the contradiction or resolution failure without being told an entire author or work is fabricated.

## Inputs, outputs and boundaries

**Inputs:** Content and a local reference corpus.

**Outputs:** Separate contradiction or unresolved-reference findings with claim spans, reference evidence and declared resolution scope.

**Limits:** An incomplete corpus cannot prove a citation does not exist; A contradiction can reflect an incorrect reference; No intent or dishonesty finding

## Method and evidence development

- Separate contradiction and unresolved-citation evidence types.
- Implement passage comparison and reproducible citation-resolution scope.
- Test fictional examples, quotations, incomplete corpora and legitimate but absent references.

## Acceptance and error boundaries

- Blind labels distinguish contradiction from missing evidence.
- Offline non-resolution alone never establishes that a source does not exist anywhere.
- Publish error rates by evidence type; NF and FI may describe different claims in one answer.

Numeric thresholds, supported languages/subjects and hardware/latency requirements are defined and accepted for this claim before independent evaluation; no PS threshold is borrowed by default. Existing editorial floors are not measured accuracy.
