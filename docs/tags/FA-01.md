# FA-01 — Full AI contribution provenance

Planning contract · 2026-10-08. No new runtime support, registration or release is asserted.

## Baseline job and outcome

Evaluate positive origin evidence for full AI generation within an explicitly observed or attested workflow.

The reader sees the origin assertion, source of evidence and supported observation window.

## Inputs, outputs and boundaries

**Inputs:** Positive provenance evidence and an identified artifact.

**Outputs:** A bounded full-generation provenance assertion identifying its source records, observation window and trust limits.

**Limits:** Missing human evidence must never imply AI origin; Typing patterns, disability, language style, or repetition are not origin proof; A trusted signature is evidence of its signer’s statement, not independent truth

## Method and evidence development

- Define acceptable generation events and manifest claims with signer trust.
- Verify provenance binding, continuity and ambiguous/missing-history handling.
- Test copied human text, spoofed manifests, missing events and transformed subjects.

## Acceptance and error boundaries

- Absent typing, paste bursts or missing human evidence never establish FA.
- Valid signatures establish the signer/record, not the truth of its origin claim.
- Independent provenance cases include assisted-input users and incomplete capture.

Numeric thresholds, supported languages/subjects and hardware/latency requirements are defined and accepted for this claim before independent evaluation; no PS threshold is borrowed by default. Existing editorial floors are not measured accuracy.

## Prerequisites and failure

WP-PROTOCOL, WP-VALIDATION, WP-UX, WP-INTEGRITY supply only the necessary primitives for this selected claim/route, not completion of unrelated tags.

Withhold or disable only the affected claim/route. Required auth, privacy or integrity failures must not be bypassed. Independent routes stay available where their own prerequisites are met.
