# MT-01 — Manipulative framing

Planning contract · 2026-10-08. No new runtime support, registration or release is asserted.

## Baseline job and outcome

Evaluate contextual pressure, constrained-choice framing and unsupported authority appeals.

The reader sees the framing and relevant context, with a measured disagreement/uncertainty boundary.

## Inputs, outputs and boundaries

**Inputs:** Content and its relevant context.

**Outputs:** Contextual framing findings, matched evidence and the versioned annotation/error policy used to interpret them.

**Limits:** Urgency and persuasion can be legitimate; Context and disagreement must be reported; The MT-specific human-agreement gate must not block other tags

## Method and evidence development

- Write an annotation guide separating persuasion, urgent safety advice and coercive framing.
- Implement indicator/context evidence and a dismissible explanation.
- Obtain blind multiple-human labels and adjudicate disagreements.

## Acceptance and error boundaries

- Report agreement, category errors and confidence calibration separately.
- Existing alpha 0.55 and accuracy values remain candidates until the protocol is accepted.
- MT disagreement never blocks PS, provenance or privacy tag release.

Numeric thresholds, supported languages/subjects and hardware/latency requirements are defined and accepted for this claim before independent evaluation; no PS threshold is borrowed by default. Existing editorial floors are not measured accuracy.
