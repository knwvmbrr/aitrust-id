# BT-01 — Positive automation-evidence proposal

Planning contract · 2026-10-08. No new runtime support, registration or release is asserted.

**Decision gate:** Owner/RFC acceptance of this proposal before registration or runtime issuance.

## Baseline job and outcome

Research positive automation evidence without judging people from style, speed or absent human signals.

A finding reports supported automation evidence within a defined workflow, without labeling an ordinary person a bot.

## Inputs, outputs and boundaries

**Inputs:** Positive, consented evidence within a defined observation scope.

**Outputs:** A proposed positive-automation observation with evidence/capture scope; no inferred automation from absent human signals.

**Limits:** Templates, repetition, timing, and learned writing patterns are not automation proof; Absent human evidence never becomes a bot verdict; Does not ship if indicators cannot separate ordinary human behavior

## Method and evidence development

- Define positive, verifiable automation evidence and its capture boundaries.
- Test human templates, support scripts, second-language writing and assistive input as controls.
- Measure whether candidate indicators discriminate; abandon unsupported inference methods while preserving the research record.

## Acceptance and error boundaries

- Absence of human evidence never issues BT.
- Repetition, template structure and timing regularity alone are not accepted proof.
- No deployment if the proposed evidence cannot support the declared claim.

Numeric thresholds, supported languages/subjects and hardware/latency requirements are defined and accepted for this claim before independent evaluation; no PS threshold is borrowed by default. Existing editorial floors are not measured accuracy.
